# Maple Retail Group purchase orders (fictional)

Every page and every email is fictional test data. Maple Retail Group Inc. (customer MAPLE-01) buys from Northern Foods
Manufacturing Inc. Generated by `python3 scripts/maple_pos.py` (needs reportlab only).
`expected-results.json` is hand-written from the acceptance checks in docs/04-acceptance.md and is run by
`frontend/lib/maple-pack.test.ts`. It is never regenerated.

## The two demo orders (served by the app)
| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| PO-2026-104620 (Milk) | Every check passes; Review-all holds it for one approval; then ERP, confirmation, ship notice, invoice |
| PO-2026-104633 (Cheese) | Cheddar printed above the ERP price (decision needed); Mozzarella short in ERP stock (split and backorder; retailer accepts) |
