# Fictional scenario: Maple Retail Group and Northern Foods (D75)

One retailer, one supplier, all fictional and labelled so on every page. Maple Retail Group Inc. (customer MAPLE-01, ship-to MAPLE-DC-01, store banner FreshMart Markets) orders from Northern Foods Manufacturing Inc., a dairy supplier whose ERP holds the agreed prices, minimums and stock. Printed documents are US purchase orders in USD, cases of 12.

Two purchase orders are served by the app as sample emails. Both are in `fixtures/maple/`, generated by `scripts/maple_pos.py`.

| PO | Items | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| PO-2026-104620 (milk) | Whole Milk 1 L, 120 cases at 18.00; Semi-Skimmed Milk 1 L, 100 cases at 17.40; freight 275.00; total 4,175.00 | Every check passes. In Review-all mode one approval sends it to the ERP. The order confirmation, ship notice and invoice follow. |
| PO-2026-104633 (cheese) | Cheddar Block 400 g, 60 cases at 54.60 (agreed price 52.80); Mozzarella 250 g, 85 cases at 38.40; freight 275.00; total 6,815.00 | One check fails: the Cheddar price. A person accepts the printed price with a reason and the order goes to the ERP. Mozzarella is short (60 on hand, 40 due on 19 November), so a person chooses to ship now and backorder the rest. The retailer sees the revised confirmation and accepts it. |

Single-condition test orders live in `fixtures/maple/tests/` (one acceptance check each): Mozzarella below the minimum of 10 cases (PO-2026-104701), a retired Cheddar GTIN sent four times to prove held, candidate-only, reuse and revoked (104702 to 104705), milk ordered in each with the factor 12 (104706), another buyer with the same retired GTIN (104707), a ship-to not on file (104708), a Mozzarella-only order that is short on stock (104709) and a revision of the milk PO (PO-2026-104620-r2, line 1 from 120 to 150 cases).

Expected results are written by hand in `fixtures/maple/expected-results.json` (cases M-01 to M-09) and run by `frontend/lib/maple-pack.test.ts`. The intake test pack in `fixtures/test-pack/` (TP-01 to TP-18) uses the same retailer and items. Totals are decimal. GTINs sit on the GS1 example company prefix 0614141.
